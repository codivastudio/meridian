05 / THE HOUSE

Style is in the details.

MERIDIAN was founded on a simple frustration: most watches shout when they should speak quietly. We build for men who don't need to prove anything, and who notice everything.

Every case, clasp, bracelet link and leather stitch is considered twice — once for how it looks today, and once for how it will look in ten years.
